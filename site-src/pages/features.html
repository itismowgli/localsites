Safety and privacy

11

What it does with your computer, and what it won't do.

  • No telemetryNo accounts, no tracking, no upsells.
  • Certificate limited to .testThe certificate authority can only sign .test and localhost, never real websites.
  • Only your computerEvery service listens on 127.0.0.1.
  • Owner-only controlThe app, CLI and agents talk over a socket only your account can open.
  • The app proves who it isOn macOS, LocalSites checks the code signature of whoever is asking.
  • Passwords in the KeychainOr the Secret Service on Linux. Never on a command line.
  • Backups before anything riskyIf a backup fails, nothing is changed.
  • Problem reports are offTurn them on if you like; you see each report, cleaned, before it's sent.
  • Trash, not deleteProjects go to the Trash so you can put them back.
  • .env backups kept outsideThey hold passwords, so they never sit in a folder that's served or committed.
  • Strict app securityOnly the app's own code runs in its window.
