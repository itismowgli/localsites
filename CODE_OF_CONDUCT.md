# Code of Conduct

LocalSites follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work,
not the person. Harassment, insults, and sharing other people's private information are not accepted
in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report problems privately to the maintainers (see SECURITY.md for how to reach them). Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or ban accounts that break these rules.
