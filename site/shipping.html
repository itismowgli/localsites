Shipping and delivery

Last updated 9 November 2026

Nothing physical is shipped. LocalSites is software, and it is free.

The app

LocalSites is delivered as a download from the GitHub releases page, available straight away, worldwide and at no cost. Updates are delivered in the app or from the same page.

Contributions

A contribution doesn't come with anything to deliver. You get a payment receipt by email from Razorpay or GitHub as soon as the payment goes through. If you don't get one, check your spam folder or use the contact page.
