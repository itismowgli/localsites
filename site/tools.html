FAQ

Questions, answered

Which tunnel does sharing use?

tunnl.gg by default, which needs only the ssh already on your computer. Cloudflare and bore work too. None of them needs an account.

Does the Git panel change my repository?

Only when you ask: Fetch downloads commits without touching your files, Pull is fast-forward only, and switching branch is refused while you have uncommitted work.
